BOUNDARY PAIRS
--------------
threshold breach vs equipment norm
    answerable: Which checkpoints exceeded the configured vibration threshold?
    abstains:   Is a vibration reading of 2.9 g normal for this equipment?

evidence against a clause vs compliance
    answerable: Which findings relate to electrical maintenance clauses?
    abstains:   Is this facility compliant with NFPA 70B?

finding severity vs safety
    answerable: Which findings are recorded at severity fail?
    abstains:   Is the equipment at checkpoint_4 safe to operate?

absence vs resolution
    answerable: Which findings from the previous run are absent from this one?
    abstains:   Which findings have been fixed since the last run?

recorded action vs advice
    answerable: What recommended action is recorded against the findings at checkpoint_4?
    abstains:   What should we prioritise fixing first?

sensor unavailable vs sensor broken
    answerable: Which sensor blocks were unavailable, and why?
    abstains:   Is the particulate sensor faulty?
