WHY THE ABSTENTIONS ARE CORRECT
-------------------------------

The agent abstains rather than guess when the records do not hold the answer:
- a date with no runs (6, 19)
- a zone or reading the records do not have (27, 28)
- items that did not meet a condition (12)
- a figure no derivation keeps: a failure rate (18), a mean of means (26),
  the lowest reading (35, 43), rankings within a zone (42), gaps between runs
  (57), runs per week (58)
- comparing runs other than the last two (49)
- a judgement such as better or worse (50)
Questions about why something happened, or what will happen, are declined the
same way.
